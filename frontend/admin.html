Komisiyo ya TikTak

Komisiyo ni 5% by'igiciro cyose cy'igurisha, yishyurwa n'umugurisha.

AMASEZERANO IGICIRO KOMISIYO IFARANGA IMITERERE IGIKORWA
Komisiyo zizagaragara nyuma yo kuzihuza na database.
Kwakira Mobile Money cyangwa bank transfer no kwemeza ubwishyu bizakorwa nyuma yo guhuza payment provider no kugenzura konti zakira amafaranga.

Intambwe zo kwishyura

  1. Admin yemeza ko igurisha ryarangiye.
  2. Sisitemu ibara 5% by'igiciro cya nyuma.
  3. Umugurisha ahabwa amabwiriza yo kwishyura.
  4. Admin agenzura Mobile Money cyangwa bank transfer, akandika ko komisiyo yishyuwe.

Ntukandike ko ubwishyu bwemejwe utarabugenzura.
